## Privacy draft

**Introduction**

> This draft explains the privacy boundaries built into the replacement
> website.

**Information this website does not collect**

> DDA Silver does not provide a website enquiry form, cart, checkout,
> saved-product area, or member profile.
>
> The website does not ask visitors to submit names, phone numbers, email
> addresses, passwords, payment information, or shipping addresses.

**Analytics choices**

> Analytics and advertising storage are denied until a visitor makes a consent
> choice. Preferences can be changed from the footer.
>
> Configured analytics events describe actions such as catalog search, filters,
> and outbound link clicks. Event payloads do not include search text, names,
> phone numbers, or email addresses.

**External services**

> WhatsApp, phone, maps, app stores, DDAJewels authentication, Sanity, and
> live-rate services are separate services with their own privacy practices.

## Website terms draft

**Catalog information**

> The website is a digital showroom. Images and descriptions help customers
> discover designs, but they are not an offer to sell online.
>
> Product appearance can vary with photography, lighting, display settings,
> and handcrafted details.

**Availability and pricing**

> Prices and inventory are intentionally not displayed. Customers should
> confirm current details directly with the showroom before relying on a
> product listing.

**External links**

> Links to WhatsApp, maps, app stores, DDAJewels, and other services open
> external platforms. Their availability and terms are controlled by those
> platforms.

## Cookie policy draft

**Essential storage**

> The consent choice is stored in the browser so the website can remember it.
> Secure, HttpOnly cookies are used during shared-account login and for the DDA
> Silver session.

**Optional analytics and advertising**

> Google analytics and advertising storage are denied by default. Visitors can
> allow or refuse each category and can reopen preferences from the website
> footer.

**Changing your choice**

> Use the Cookie preferences control in the footer at any time. A new choice
> replaces the earlier stored preference.

## Rates disclaimer draft

The rates implementation is being simplified to reuse the DDAJewels-hosted
silver rates experience. This wording must be rechecked after that work lands.

**Source and timing**

> DDAJewels remains the source of truth for customer and market-reference
> rates. DDA Silver does not calculate, edit, or substitute rate values.
>
> Network delays, market closure, data-source interruptions, or device
> conditions can make a displayed value stale or unavailable.

**No transaction guarantee**

> A displayed rate does not guarantee a transaction price or product
> availability. Confirm applicable rates and terms directly with the showroom.

**Unavailable states**

> If no valid snapshot is available, the website intentionally shows dashes
> and an unavailable message rather than zero or an estimated value.
