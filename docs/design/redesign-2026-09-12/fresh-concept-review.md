## Required refinements and limitations

- The header must ultimately use the exact existing DDA logo asset; a generated likeness is not a replacement logo.
- The hero image depicts an imagined display setting. Stone, arch, and foliage are concept staging, not photographs of the real showroom. Do not describe them as the business premises.
- Product imagery in generated concepts may differ from the real inventory. Implementation must use original approved photographs with full object framing and complete watermarks. Do not publish generated product renderings as catalog assets.
- Add a clear “Explore products” action to the opening composition. The initial generated image has navigation and catalog access but no sufficiently clear primary hero action.
- Keep all 11 real categories reachable. The visible short catalog index is not the complete taxonomy.
- Keep the actual enquiry-led model. No bag, cart, checkout, fictional reviews, or invented promotions.
- Keep the historical-versus-live distinction on rates and product prices. Any sample figures must be identified as examples in the mockup review, not quoted as current values.
