# Candidate review

> Superseded: the user rejected the incremental redesign approach. Both improvement passes, including the former B recommendation, are rejected. The sections below are historical review notes. The current direction is Version 2 of `design-language.md`; new work uses fresh 12ui drafts without the existing website as a visual reference.

## First 12ui pass — reviewed, not selected

Luna generated four homepage alternatives with the current screenshot as a reference, retaining its assets and content. The root reviewer inspected all four original PNGs.

All four retained most of the current homepage geometry: a long display heading on the left, a product hero on the right, pill-shaped controls, and a large multi-row category area. The changes were primarily surface styling and photographic staging, rather than the shorter introduction and earlier catalog access specified by the design language.

The generated product pictures also introduced surrounding marble, flowers, bowls, or cloth. Those are not approved replacement product assets. The real photographs and approved ivory backgrounds remain the implementation source of truth.

No first-pass candidate was recommended for extension. A second draw was requested with assets retained but layout and wording free to change, while retaining the no-invention constraint. The revised brief explicitly calls for a shorter headline, compact category navigation, an earlier featured-products row, and small-radius controls.

The first-pass originals remain in the 12ui run history. They are exploration evidence, not the selected redesign.

## Second 12ui pass — candidate B recommended

The root reviewer inspected all four revised originals. Candidate B is the strongest working direction: its shorter introduction, more compact category row, and featured-product images create the clearest improvement in browsing density. Candidate A gives more space back to the headline; C moves featured products farther down; D introduces category groupings that do not match the current inventory taxonomy.

Candidate B is a designer recommendation for extension and user review, not an approved production design.

Known differences from the written brief:

- The raster generation approximates product photography and the monogram. Implementation must use the exact existing assets, including the complete approved watermark, rather than extracting newly drawn objects from the mockup.
- Primary controls still use pill shapes. The written system specifies small corner radii; this remains a refinement to make if this direction is approved.
- The visible category strip contains six entries. All 11 real categories must remain discoverable through accessible overflow or an explicit all-categories control.
- Featured photographs need real product names, specifications, and price states in the continuation. An image-only strip is not the complete catalog-card design.
- The cream surface texture is a generated treatment. Production should start with the solid proposed paper token and avoid large background raster files solely to recreate subtle noise.

The lower-page, mobile, catalog, detail, rates, and supporting-page mockups will be assessed against these constraints as they become available.
